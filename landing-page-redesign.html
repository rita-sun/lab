Landing Page Redesign

Redesigned a barcode software landing page for Clear Spider, focusing on clearer information structure, accessibility, and a stronger path from problem to solution.

Figma FigJam Illustrator Unbounce
The Problem

Clear Spider's barcode software landing page was outdated and no longer reflected the design, accessibility, and conversion expectations of a modern B2B SaaS website.

I redesigned the page to create a clearer information structure, improve accessibility, and make the path from the user's problem to the product's solution easier to follow.

Defining a Roadmap

I started by creating a project roadmap to define the goals, deliverables, and timeline. This helped break the project into smaller stages and keep the work moving through research, wireframing, and implementation.

Research

I researched B2B SaaS landing page patterns and competitor websites to understand how similar products communicated their value. I looked at layout, messaging, calls to action, and how information was prioritized throughout the page.

I then used the research to create a content outline that defined what information the page needed and how it could be organized.

Wireframing

I translated the content strategy into wireframes, focusing on the order of information and creating a clear transition from the user's problem to the product's solution.

I went through multiple iterations and incorporated team feedback to refine the layout and emphasis of the key messages.

Some of the main changes included combining problem and feature content into a more direct problem-solution narrative, removing metric icons to keep attention on key numbers, and adjusting text and interactive colours for better accessibility.

Building in Unbounce

I built the final landing page in Unbounce, translating the approved design into a responsive page for both desktop and mobile.

I also applied accessibility practices such as alt text and WCAG-compliant colour contrast, while keeping layers organized and labeled so the page could be updated more easily in the future.

A second page variant was also created to support A/B testing of form placement, giving the team a way to compare different approaches over time.

The Final Page

The final design brought the page into a more modern visual direction while making the information easier to scan and the product's value easier to understand.

What I Learned

This project helped me understand how research and content structure can shape the design of a B2B SaaS landing page. I learned to think about the page as a problem-to-solution flow rather than designing each section independently.

Iterating on wireframes and then building the page directly in Unbounce also showed me how feedback and implementation constraints can influence the final design.
