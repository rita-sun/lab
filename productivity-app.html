Productivity App

A study group accountability app designed to help students keep track of classes, assignments, and weekly meetings while encouraging consistency and collaboration.

Figma
The Problem

During the study term, my friends and I noticed that it was becoming harder to keep up with classes and stay productive. Missing classes made it easier to fall behind, which often led to cramming before midterms and exams.

I wanted to design a system that could help a small group of students stay accountable to each other throughout the week.

The Idea

I explored a study group app that would bring classes, weekly tasks, meetings, and group communication into one place.

A key part of the concept was accountability. Members could see what needed to be completed each week and use playful punishments to keep the group motivated when someone fell behind.

Initial Ideas

I started by brainstorming the different features that could support a study group and keep members accountable.

Study Group

  • Invite members to a study group
  • Assign admin privileges
  • Edit and delete old posts
  • Approve schedules

Class Schedule

  • Check in to classes
  • Track topics covered each week
  • Assign members to each class

Weekly Tasks

  • Assign homework and readings
  • Track completion
  • Discuss tasks with the group

Weekly Meetings

  • Schedule group meetings
  • Meeting reminders
  • Shared notes

Messaging

  • Group chat
  • Individual messaging
  • Separate task-based groups

Punishments

  • Add a date and description
  • Add photos or videos
  • Archive past punishments
Narrowing the Idea

From the brainstorming stage, I focused on the features that felt most important to the core experience: study groups, weekly schedules and tasks, messaging, and the punishment system.

Other ideas such as alarms and a bulletin board felt less essential for a first version and could be added later.

Visual Direction

I explored the colour palette and typography before building out the interface, aiming for a visual style that felt approachable and lighthearted rather than like a traditional productivity tool.

Wireframes + Final Design

I developed the app in Figma, starting with wireframes and gradually refining the layouts into a more complete interface. I focused on making the main flows easy to understand while keeping the different group features connected.

What I Learned

This project helped me build my Figma and wireframing skills while learning how to think through an app from the user's perspective.

Working independently also pushed me to prioritize features and think more carefully about how different parts of a product fit together.
